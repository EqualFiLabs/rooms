Governance tests exercise immutable disabled-by-default configurations, widened BPS/room/duration bounds, permanent asset history, versioned integrations, capacity, independent roles and pause-only guardian.

Settlement tests exercise the selected target only, proof failure rollback, cached zero randomness, posting after effective close, chain/Diamond/round domain separation and complete populated/empty outcomes. Independent configurations settle in reverse order. Claims and revenue suites cover one-shot consumption, all/partial/zero prize expiry, exact 30-day boundaries, false/reverting token calls and retry, cross-facet reentrancy, valid chosen receivers, pause and asset removal, aggregated finalizer credits, historical Router buckets, exact temporary allowance and reset, donations, shortfalls, known-token admission and forced-native delivery.
